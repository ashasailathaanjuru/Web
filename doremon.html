ABOUT DORAEMON

Doraemon is a famous Japanese cartoon character. He is a robotic cat from the 22nd century. Doraemon comes to the past to help Nobita Nobi.

ABOUT DORAEMON

Doraemon has a magical four-dimensional pocket. He keeps many futuristic gadgets inside his pocket. He uses these gadgets to help Nobita solve his problems.
